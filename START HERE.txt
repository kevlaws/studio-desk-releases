STUDIO DESK 1.0.0 — QUICK START
Original RØDECaster Pro • Windows x64 • Optional RSS news

WINDOWS ICON
The app uses the neon red ON AIR sign in Explorer, the taskbar and window titles.
Extract this release to its own folder and open StudioDesk.exe. Existing notes,
jingles, settings and screen arrangements are kept in the same local profile.
If a pinned shortcut still opens an older version, unpin that shortcut and pin
this version's StudioDesk.exe. No administrator access or .NET install is needed.

REMEMBERING YOUR SCREENS
Choose a view and monitor for each window in Screens. Each window automatically
remembers its view, monitor, normal size/position, full-screen, maximised and
always-on-top settings. Use Quit Studio Desk in Screens, or close the main
window, to save and close the entire app. The arrangement returns next launch.

Closing an additional window also keeps its saved screen. Reopen it through
Screens, or simply restart the app. Remove saved screen is the explicit option
for forgetting a window. Minimising a window does not erase its saved placement.
The Full screen checkboxes in Screens show each window's current setting.

ONE STUDIO DASHBOARD ON EVERY MONITOR
The regular Studio view now uses the same dashboard and toolbar in the main
window and every additional window. It keeps the original arrangement: clock,
ON AIR and timer above the channel strips, then jingles beside show notes, with
stereo meters on the right. Moving windows or changing monitors only scales it.

Every Studio toolbar includes Screens, Stop All, Demo, Sync desk, Full screen,
News, Customise and Setup. Weather is in the connection bar. Clock selection,
channel renaming, timer Set / Start / Reset, jingle Manage and the meter Source
button are the same everywhere. The news ticker also has the same Pause,
Refresh and Settings controls. Full screen acts on the window you are using.

Use Screens to choose each window's view and monitor. The windows share one
mixer connection, audio output, active bank, show timer, clock mode and notes.
Channel names and visibility apply to all Studio windows. Each window remembers
its own view, position, full-screen and always-on-top setting.

Studio display remains the separate giant clock, ON AIR and weather view.
Its toolbar hides only in full screen; Escape or F11 brings it back. Regular
Studio keeps its setup toolbar visible in full screen.

STUDIO DISPLAY WEATHER
Studio display keeps the giant clock on the left. The right column is split
equally: ON AIR at the top, with large weather conditions and forecast symbols
below. It uses your existing weather location (Newcastle upon Tyne by default).
Change the location through Weather or a Presenter panel's Location button.
Weather unavailable/cached labels and the source credit remain visible.

VISUAL WEATHER AND STUDIO DISPLAY
Presenter weather uses the full card: a large condition symbol and temperature,
plus three forecast tiles with symbols, daily highs/lows and descriptions.
The symbols are original vector artwork and remain sharp on large screens.
Unavailable or unknown weather is not assigned an invented sun/cloud symbol.
The existing location, source attribution and cached-data labels are retained.

Choose the fifth view, Studio display, for a giant clock and ON AIR lamp.
It is available in the main view selector and in every additional screen window.
Screens lets you assign it to any monitor. F11 enters a clean full-screen view
with the clock, lamp and weather; Escape or F11 restores the toolbar. The display
uses your shared Digital, Circular or Auto-switch clock preference.
The lamp is red when a selected microphone is known live, grey otherwise.
Sync needed and Mixer disconnected remain visible when required. Jingle keys
are ignored in Studio display, which is intended as a passive studio screen.
Your other windows, audio playback and shared mixer connection continue normally.

VIEWS, WEATHER AND MULTIPLE MONITORS
Choose Studio, Jingle board, Prep desk or Presenter from the top dropdown.
Screens > Add display window opens another view. Choose its view and monitor,
or drag the window to the screen you want. Use F11 for full screen in that
window. Views, placement and always-on-top preferences survive restart.
The main window closes the whole session; closing an additional window removes
that window from the saved layout. Up to six additional windows are supported.
All windows share ONE MIDI connection, jingle playback engine, active bank,
show timer and notes. Switching views or closing an extra window does not stop
jingles. STOP ALL is available in the working views; Studio display hides its
toolbar in full screen. Keys 1-8 only act in an active working view and are
suppressed in text boxes. Physical pad presses are handled once.

Weather defaults to Newcastle upon Tyne. Click weather / Location to search
for another city, hide weather or refresh. Current model conditions and a
three-day forecast use Celsius, mph and UK time; they refresh every 15 minutes.
Weather data is from Open-Meteo, with dated cached results if offline.

Prep desk uses BBC UK news, BBC Tyne local news, Guardian music headlines,
Digital Spy entertainment headlines, and Wikipedia events / notable birthdays.
Today selects a few items from every category. + Notes appends the item's
category, date, title and source URL to the shared, autosaved show notes.
Click a prep headline or a scrolling ticker headline to open its article in
your browser. Category badges still open the source homepage.
News feeds use the publishers' publication dates when available. Wikipedia
birthdays show birth years, never an assumed current age. Check linked source
material before reading on air. Dated prep content rolls over at midnight.
Prep fetches begin when you open Prep desk and refresh every 15 minutes during
the session. Weather and prep require internet for updates; mixer, playback,
notes and timers keep working offline. Failed sources do not block others.
Presenter includes shared notes, clock, ON AIR, weather, show timer, time to
the next hour, channels and stereo meters. Automatic current/next-track
integration is deferred until your online playout system's API is available.

COLOURFUL PADS AND ON AIR LAMP
Jingle slots 1–8 are red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple and pink,
matching your RØDECaster photo. The same slot colours apply to every bank.
A playing pad keeps its colour with a white outline and dark PLAYING badge.
The large ON AIR lamp is red when microphones are known live, grey otherwise.
OFF AIR is confirmed. The small Sync needed button means microphone status is
unknown; click it for Sync desk, or hover to see which microphones need checking.
Mixer disconnected means MIDI is unavailable; click it to open Setup.
Grey alone does not establish that microphones are off. Demo is labelled.
The decorative dots, repeated heading and always-visible footnote were removed
The lamp's tooltip explains how its status follows the mixer.

COPY YOUR EXISTING RØDECASTER BANKS
In RØDE's Companion App, choose Save Show to create a .rcpshow backup.
In Studio Desk, select Manage > Copy from RØDE Show and select that file.
Review the banks, then Copy banks into Studio Desk. New banks are added; your
existing app banks and all sounds/settings on the mixer stay intact.
The same backup cannot be added twice. A new backup adds separate copies.
Names, pad order and loop flags are copied; hardware bank switching is not synced.
Use the dashboard's bank selector to choose the corresponding bank manually.

Copied banks default to hardware-triggered app playback OFF. Your physical pads
continue playing their original sounds on the mixer. Clicking the app pads or
using keys 1–8 plays the PC copies through the selected USB output.
To use physical pads to trigger PC copies instead, enable the per-bank option
in Manage, match the bank manually and lower the mixer's SOUND PADS fader.
Stop All stops app jingles only; it does not stop the mixer's own sounds.

Some special pads may not have exported audio. These retain their name and slot
with ON RØDECASTER ONLY; the !@#* pad in your backup is one such pad.
Special hardware pad actions are not recreated in Studio Desk. Exported
audio uses normal app play/stop behaviour; relevant pads show an explanatory note.
The original Show backup is read-only throughout the copy process.

CHANNEL LABELS AND COLOURS
Click a channel card to rename it, then Save label. Enter saves; Escape cancels.
Or open Customise to edit all eight labels, for example Presenter and Guest,
and choose which channels to show. Labels are remembered after restarting.
Red LIVE = known raised and unmuted. Yellow MUTED = confirmed mute active.
Dark maroon OFF = known fader down, without a known active mute.
Unsynced and disconnected channels keep explicit neutral status cards.
Sound pads has no mute mapping: its raised fader is red and says FADER UP.
Percentages and thin lines show fader positions, not measured audio levels.

1. INSTALL OR EXTRACT AND OPEN
Run StudioDesk-1.0.0-Setup-x64.exe to install for the current user. Setup adds a
Start menu entry, offers a desktop shortcut and includes an uninstaller.
Or extract the portable ZIP to a folder you want to keep and open StudioDesk.exe.
No account, subscription, administrator installation or separate .NET runtime is needed.

2. CONNECT YOUR RØDECASTER
Power it on and connect USB. On the RØDECaster, enable:
Settings > Advanced > Audio > Operations > MIDI Control.
Open Setup in Studio Desk. Select RODECaster Pro MIDI and Connect / resync.
If another program owns the MIDI port, close its MIDI connection and retry.

3. VERIFY MAPPINGS ONCE (FIRST SETUP)
In Setup, select which channels count as microphones (Mic 1–4 initially).
Open Sync desk. All eight channels are here, including USB, Phone, Bluetooth
and Sound pads, even if you have hidden them from the dashboard.
For each channel whose mute mapping is unverified:
- Click Learn mute on that channel's row.
- Press that physical channel's MUTE button once.
- Wait for Mute mapping saved, then match its physical MUTE light using Light ON/OFF.
  The learning press changes the actual hardware mute state.
Mic 1 and all eight fader/pad defaults were observed on the development test desk.
Verify these on your own device; use Learn if a control does not match.
Learn fader / Learn pad are available for correcting mappings if necessary.
Mappings are saved. You do not need to relearn them every time you start.
Learning one control preserves the other channels' states.
The Sound pads row is fader-only. Its eight separate button triggers are below,
with press counts so you can test them here. The app does not play jingles from
hardware pad presses while this window is open; the mixer's own sounds can play.

4. CONFIRM CURRENT STATE (AFTER START / USB RECONNECTION)
The original model reports a mute press/release, not the resulting on/off state.
It cannot tell this app whether controls changed while the app was closed.
Previous known values are saved as references, separately from live state.
Open Sync desk and compare the Previous column with the physical controls.
If they still match, click "Previous values still match · confirm now" once.
This fills compatible unknown values only; fresh readings are kept.
If they differ, move the relevant faders and match each physical MUTE light with
Light ON / Light OFF. Do this once initially to establish the saved references.

The confirmation button is disabled if offline, in demo, or no saved values apply.
Do not guess a mute state. Sync needed means the status is unknown.
The red ON AIR light means at least one selected mic is known raised and unmuted.
OFF AIR means all selected mics are known down or muted.
This describes mixer controls, not whether your station is broadcasting.
Use ordinary main-mix mode; producer/talkback and pre-fader recording are not tracked.
Resync whenever a mute changes through the touchscreen, or whenever the display and hardware disagree.

CUSTOMISE YOUR DASHBOARD
Click Customise to hide unused channels. The remaining cards compact into fewer
rows and the jingle/notes area expands. "Include in ON AIR" is separate from
visibility: hidden microphones can still keep the ON AIR light on.
Click the small clock button to choose Digital, Circular or Auto-switch.
Digital and Circular stay fixed. Auto-switch starts with Circular and changes
views every 15 seconds; the button shows the next view and seconds remaining.
The former Alternate setting is now called Auto-switch; saved choices migrate.
You can hide the headphone panel or stereo meters to reclaim their space.
Hiding the headphones expands ON AIR into that space with larger lettering.

STEREO AUDIO METERS
The right-hand meters show actual input sample peaks, not MIDI fader positions.
By default, the app selects the connected RODECaster Stereo recording input.
In Customise, select your input and Start selected meter. Hover the meter panel
to see its source name. USB routing / mix-minus affects which sounds reach it.
The white line holds a peak briefly; green/yellow/red segments show dBFS level.
No audio is written to disk and no input is fed back to the speakers.
If the input disappears, the meters clear. Reconnect and restart the selected
meter in Customise. They never switch to another microphone automatically.

HEADPHONE GAUGES
The four circular gauges are prepared for MIDI headphone knob positions.
Their real mappings on the original RODECaster remain UNVERIFIED. A dash means
unknown, not zero. To test support, click Learn knob in Customise and turn only
that physical knob gradually. Learning requires changing CC values and rejects
signals already assigned to other controls. If no usable MIDI arrives within
30 seconds, the gauge remains unknown. The app cannot read an analog knob that
does not send MIDI. Do not confuse these rings with the stereo audio peak meter.

5. SET UP JINGLE AUDIO
In Setup, choose the RØDECaster USB / Stereo playback output and click Arm selected output.
The USB fader controls sounds played from the computer.
Keep the hardware SOUND PADS fader down when using its pads to trigger app jingles.
This prevents also hearing the RØDECaster's own stored pad sounds.
Save and close Setup. Verify the route at a comfortable monitoring level before a show.
An unplugged/failed audio output stops all jingles. Re-arm it explicitly in Setup.
The app will never silently move playback to another Windows device.

6. IMPORT AND PLAY
Click an empty pad to import a mono/stereo WAV or MP3. Files are copied into the app library.
Give the pad a name, choose one-shot or loop and set its volume, then Save jingle.
Click a loaded pad to play; click again to stop with a short fade.
Keys 1–8 trigger the selected bank. Physical pads do so only when the bank's
hardware-trigger option in Manage is enabled (off for copied RØDE banks).
Typing in show notes does not trigger keyboard jingles. Keyboard controls work while Studio Desk is focused.
Right-click a pad to edit. Manage creates or renames banks.
Changing bank leaves already playing sounds running; STOP ALL stops every bank.
Hardware bank changes do not select a Studio Desk bank.
Hardware triggers are suppressed while a setup or editing dialog is open.

7. CLOCK, TIMER AND DISPLAY
The clock follows the PC's local time. Set the Windows time zone correctly.
Set chooses Countdown or Stopwatch.
Countdown has separate HOURS, MINUTES and SECONDS fields. Hours: 00–99;
minutes and seconds: 00–59. The minimum countdown is one second.
Stopwatch starts at 00:00:00 and counts upwards in hours, minutes and seconds.
Both use total hours, so a stopwatch does not wrap back to zero after 24 hours.
Apply and reset pauses the timer. Start / Pause / Reset controls it on the dashboard.
The mode and countdown duration are remembered, but running progress is not
restored after closing the app. Cancel leaves the current timer unchanged.
Countdowns show overtime visually; they do not play an alarm.
F11 toggles full screen. Escape leaves full screen.
Ctrl+Space stops all jingles when you are not typing in a text field.
Setup selects the monitor, station name and optional always-on-top mode.
Window placement, banks and notes are remembered.

8. DEMO AND TROUBLESHOOTING
Demo lets you inspect the dashboard with the RØDECaster switched off.
The amber DEMO banner clearly distinguishes simulation; audio is disabled.
Exit demo to return to live input, open Sync desk, then re-arm audio in Setup.
Setup > MIDI diagnostics shows the last 400 received messages and exports a text log.
No MIDI events? Check the USB cable, MIDI Control setting, selected port and other MIDI apps.
Missing sound? Check output armed, correct endpoint, USB fader, pad volume and file assignment.

9. MULTIPLE NEWS FEEDS
Open News or Settings on the ticker to add up to eight RSS / Atom feeds.
Use the presets for BBC News, BBC North East and Digital Spy, or Custom feed.
North East adds both Tyne and Tees, labelled LOCAL NEWS. Digital Spy is labelled
ENTERTAINMENT. Edit any label and its colour; tick to enable, use the arrows to
reorder, or Remove. Save and apply commits changes; Cancel discards edits.
Your previous single-feed address and preferences are preserved on upgrade.
Up to 12 stories per feed alternate between enabled sources, with duplicate
headlines skipped. A label appears before each story, alongside source credit.
Pause / Resume holds the text; hovering also pauses it temporarily. Refresh
fetches all enabled sources independently. Click a badge to open its source.
Refresh defaults to 10 minutes (adjustable 5–60); speed and visibility are saved.
News uses the internet. MIDI, timers, notes and jingles still work offline.
If one feed fails, the others continue. Cached stories show CACHED on their
badge and their original fetch time on hover. News settings show each feed's
status. A failed feed with no cache contributes no stories until it recovers.
Hide the ticker in News settings to regain its space and stop feed requests.

YOUR DATA
%LOCALAPPDATA%\StudioDesk
Back up this entire folder, including Library, to preserve your sounds and notes.
previous-mixer.json contains historical references only, never automatic live state.
The app stores no audio recordings. Enabled news, weather and show-prep features
contact their online sources; core MIDI, local audio, timers and notes work offline.
NewsCache holds separate offline copies per feed. The old news-cache.json is
retained and used only for the matching single-feed migration.
